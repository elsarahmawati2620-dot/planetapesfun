THE COLLECTION

Not an NFT mint.
A generative token.

Pieces carry identity and traits like NFTs, but the token itself is fungible and trades like an ERC-20. First on a bonding curve, then on Uniswap after graduation. Ownership of a whole $uAPES token is what mints and holds an Ape.

Art is uploaded as layers — one folder per trait, one image per variant. The protocol composes each piece. Possible looks are the product of those variants. The number of Apes that can ever exist is the supply: 3,000.

  • Nothing exists at launch. Buys mint one item per whole token received.
  • Fractions and transfers never mint. Only new buys create Apes.
  • Traits cannot be previewed or cherry-picked. Looks come from a later block.
  • Layers stay on-chain. Any Ape can be rendered from chain data alone.

PROTOCOL

How an Ape is born

  1. 01

    Deploy

    Name, ticker $uAPES, 3,000 supply, and curve are set permanently. Collections use 18 decimals, so one whole token always backs one item.

  2. 02

    Upload layers

    Art goes on-chain across several transactions. Once trading opens, the layer set is locked. Metadata cannot change later.

  3. 03

    Buy & reveal

    A buy mints one Ape per whole token. Traits reveal layer by layer from a later block, so rarity cannot be gamed at buy time.

  4. 04

    Graduate

    At the ETH target, anyone can graduate. Liquidity moves to Uniswap and the LP key is burned. Each Ape also trades on its own card.
